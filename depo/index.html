Depo, a squared gothic with small caps, in the line of Bank Gothic.

It grew out of the lettering I drew for the sleeve of Drenched by JRAY, and was then drawn out into a typeface: wide, square-shouldered capitals, with small caps on the lowercase keys at 80% of the cap height.

The contrast is low, as in Bank Gothic. The bars are a little lighter than the stems, the diagonals close to even, and the weight of the N sits in its diagonal. A second axis takes the contrast up, to thin bars and heavy diagonals, for display.

The name reads in Latin and in Cyrillic, where Депо is a transport depot, and the specimens stay there: night buses, last departures, the garage.

In progress: two weights in two contrasts so far, with the full Latin set to follow.
