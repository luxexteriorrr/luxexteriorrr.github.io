Media systems that see you, feel you, and respond in real time.

Sentra is not a company.

It is a project about how AI persuades: a speculative company with no product except you. It learns what you care about, then claims it has always done exactly that, and what you say in private appears on the screens beside you. Your conversation is not kept; messages go to OpenAI to write the replies and are cleared with every new chat.

Made by Kirill Konstantinov, with Anton Blackledge. MA Digital Direction, Royal College of Art, with tutors Dr Claude Dutson and Carlotta Auon. First shown at RCA2025, White City, July 2025.

What is Sentra?

We could simply tell you, but it wouldn't make sense yet. Sentra adapts—the more you share about your work, your interests, your challenges, the better Sentra can demonstrate what it actually does. Each conversation is different because each person is different. We are genuinely curious to learn about your world!

Please note: This is an experimental AI system for artistic purposes. Don't share sensitive personal information, financial details, or private data. Conversations may be used for academic research. This system is not intended for medical, legal, or professional advice.
