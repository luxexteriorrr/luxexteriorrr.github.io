      Kirill Konstantinov [Moscow, 1999] is a graphic designer and art director based in London. His work is a dialogue between research and the object being made: books, identities, typefaces and websites. He holds an MA in Digital Direction and an MRes in Communication Design, both from the Royal College of Art.

      Available for editorial design and art direction roles in London.
